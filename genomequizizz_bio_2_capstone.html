Which chemical bond stabilizes the complementary pairing between purines and pyrimidines in the interior of the double helix?

Correct Explanation

Genome Packaging & Structural Annotations

Comprehensive biological index detailing the multi-tiered structural hierarchy of eukaryotic DNA.

Level 1 • 2 nm

DNA Double Helix Chemistry

DNA consists of two antiparallel polynucleotide chains running in 5' → 3' opposing directions. The outer backbone is formed by phosphodiester bonds between deoxyribose sugars and phosphate groups. Inner nitrogenous bases bind complementary (A=T with 2 hydrogen bonds, G≡C with 3 hydrogen bonds).

  • Major Groove: 22 Å width (site of protein binding)
  • Minor Groove: 12 Å width
  • Helical pitch: 3.4 nm per complete turn (10.5 base pairs)
Level 2 • 11 nm

Nucleosome Core Octamer

The primary unit of chromatin packaging. Exactly 147 base pairs of DNA wrap 1.65 times around a basic protein core consisting of two molecules each of histones H2A, H2B, H3, and H4. Basic, positively-charged Lysine and Arginine residues electrostatically bind the negatively charged phosphate backbone of DNA.

  • Linker DNA: 20-80 bp connecting individual nucleosomes
  • Histone H1: Linker histone securing DNA entry/exit points
  • Compaction: ~6-fold reduction in length
Level 3 • 30 nm

30nm Chromatin Fiber

Nucleosomes coil into a higher-order helical solenoid or zigzag structure driven by Histone H1 interactions and N-terminal histone tail modifications. This level represents the standard resting state of interphase chromatin in eukaryotic cell nuclei.

  • Euchromatin: Less condensed, transcriptionally active
  • Heterochromatin: Highly condensed, transcriptionally silent
  • Compaction: ~40-fold reduction in length
Level 4 • 1400 nm

Metaphase Chromosome

During cell division (mitosis), 30nm fibers form radial loops anchored to a non-histone protein scaffold (Topoisomerase II & Condensin). These loops condense further into sister chromatids joined at a central centromere to permit safe genomic segregation.

  • Sister Chromatids: Duplicated identical DNA molecules
  • Centromere: Kinetochore binding site for microtubule attachment
  • Compaction: ~10,000-fold total compaction ratio

Biophysical Modeling of Eukaryotic Genome Packaging

Author: BIO 2 Research Capstone Group • Focus: Structural DNA Hierarchy & Educational Simulation

1. Executive Summary & Abstract

Human diploid cells contain approximately 2 meters of linear DNA enclosed within a nucleus measuring only 6 micrometers in diameter. This topological challenge requires a spatial compression factor of over 10,000-fold without compromising gene accessibility for transcription and DNA replication. This project presents dynamic 3D web visualizations paired with interactive retrieval practice to teach biophysical packaging mechanics.

2. Quantitative Packaging Summary Table

3. Biophysical Mechanisms

The electrostatic interaction between the negatively charged phosphate backbone ($PO_4^{3-}$) and the highly basic positively charged amino acid residues (Lysine and Arginine) on histone tails forms the thermodynamic foundation for nucleosome assembly. Reversible epigenetic modifications such as histone acetylation by Histone Acetyltransferases (HATs) neutralize positive charges on lysine residues, inducing euchromatin relaxation for active gene transcription.
